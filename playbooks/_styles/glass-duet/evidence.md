## Deviations from STYLE-COVERAGE (with reason)
- **Cadence `sc_per_10s` 4–10** instead of the table's 3.5–6: with primary captions weighted 1.0 and word-built chunks, the evidence counts ≈ 6–11 SCs per 10 s (v04 @0:00–0:10); 3.5–6 would fail every reference reel.
- **Hook alternates** add HA-07 (live rating, v04) and HA-18 (recursion, v03) to HA-05 + HA-02.
- **Sparkle bullets at 40 px** (not the 30–36 px measured): kept at the label floor without E3 because bullets carry meaning that is not always spoken; only leader labels use E3.
- **Link-card and tile rows moved above y 1500:** v05 tiles sit at y ≈ 1560 and the v04 checklist card runs to y ≈ 1585; NC-5 forbids meaning text there.

## Known gaps and inferences
- **(unverified)** speech language (English assumed), hook and CTA wording, caption chest offset (220 px inferred; the block centre y 1060–1130 is measured). Duet size ratios are now measured (audit 2026-10).
