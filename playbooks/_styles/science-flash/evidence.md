## 3. Unverified and inferred
- **(unverified) Speech language:** no transcripts; the wording is read from the burnt-in captions. All three are English.
- **(unverified) Sound:** not observable. The bed from f0 and the cue moments are the template's decision (§11), not evidence.
- **(unverified) Spoken CTA on Instagram:** the evidence is YouTube-style "SUBSCRIBE!".
- **(inferred) Anchor method:** the evidence shows marks sitting on moving targets; how they were tracked is unknown. The template uses a manual anchor pass (§17.2).
- **(inferred) Hook alternates HA-13 / HA-14:** not in the three videos (all three are HA-10); allowed as fallbacks only, with limits.
- **(inferred) P-BAND-CLIP, P-SCALE-PERSON, P-BLUR-VS-SHARP, P-MECHANISM-LOOP, P-MAP-SCHEMATIC:** created stand-ins for footage the engine can't make (CG creatures, LiDAR, microscopy, stadium CG); not in the evidence as such.

## 4. Known gaps
- The engine has no `ctx.anchor()` / `plan/anchors.json` yet (E-15): keyframes are hand-written in `scenes.js` from contact sheets.
- No B-roll bank command (E-17): `plan/broll.md` is written by hand at P1b.
- No validator check for the sticker schedule (`cta.placement: scheduled`): checked by review.
- The 3D CG sequences of the evidence (creatures, stadium, lens, transistor scale) cannot be built; FB-4 replaces them with flat diagrams, at a fidelity cost the checkpoint reports.
