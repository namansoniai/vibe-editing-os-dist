## 2. Inferred (not directly measurable)
- **Font families:** closest bundled fonts (Inter Tight for captions, Anton for grit heroes, Barlow Condensed for neon numerals, Permanent Marker for hand tags). The source uses similar but not identical faces.
- **Exact frame counts** for slams, rolls and pops are converted from 25 fps sheets sampled at 6 fps (hook) and 1 fps (body); ±2 f.
- **The matte quality** behind heroes cannot be judged from the sheets; E1 limits are taken from the registry.
- **Cadence numbers** combine the cut detector (undercounts punches) with caption swaps counted on the sheets.
- **Treatment recipes** (CSS filters) approximate the look of the clips; they are not the source's grade.

## 3. Unverified (`tokens.style.unverified`)
- `profile.language.speech` and `profile.language.captions.transform`: no transcripts; captions look like verbatim English.
- `sound.bed`: sound not observable; the bed-after-hook rule is a house default.
- `hooks.stopper.payoff_by_s`: hero land times are measured against picture only, not against the spoken word.

## 4. Known gaps and deliberate departures
- **E1 occlusion:** some evidence frames hide ≈ 40–45% of a numeral behind the head (v05 "1,20,000"); the template enforces ≥ 65% visible with the placement rules in playbook §5.2.
- **Grouping:** v01's counter writes "₹500,000" (international); the template writes Indian grouping everywhere.
- **Licensed / third-party clips** in the source (a film scene v05 @0:40, a celebrity-like still, a news-style card about another person v02 @0:06) are not reproduced; they map to created substitutes (§12.5).
- **Footage grade events and clip treatments** have no validator yet (E-16); they are built as scene overlays and checked by review.
- **Dutch roll** is capped at 4° (the source tilts ≈ 8°) to keep the corner coverage within a 1.16 crop.
