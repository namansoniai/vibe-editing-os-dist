## 2. Inferred (not directly measurable)
- **Banner font**: Montserrat Black (confirmed at full resolution 2026-10-06 by render overlay); only single words like "CHEATCODE" use a techno face.
- **Caption font**: a heavy geometric sans (Montserrat ExtraBold-like); the 720p sources can't separate Montserrat from similar faces.
- **Glow radii** (18 / 42 px) and **flood timings** (6 f) are read from 6 fps hook sheets; ±2 f.
- **Orb size and ripple** (Ø 64 → 160): from 1 fps sheets; the motion between samples is inferred.
- **Set drawing**: the evidence sets are photographic virtual backgrounds; the template paints equivalents from tokens (gradients, glow, bokeh dots), since no set photos may ship.
- **Hook SC count**: v01 and v03 carried only 2–3 events in 0–3 s; the template asks for ≥ 4 (the v02 / v04 standard).
