## Engine built-ins (template pass, engine cc324ec+)
Workarounds replaced by core features; the measured values above are now drawn as measured.
- **now built-in: T-01 whip** → `timeline.transitions` `whip` (dir right, px 60, frames 9, pre 4, blend 2) = the measured 4 f smear out + 2 f blend + 3 f smear in at ~60 px (v02 @21.3). Was: z11 isotropic `backdrop-filter` blur capped at 40 px + a 60 px ghost.
- **now built-in: T-05 zoom-blur punch** → `zoom-blur` (pre 0, frames 4, punch 0, at face): radial blur peaking on the cut into the tighter real angle, clear in 4 f (v02 @4.42). Was: z11 isotropic blur 16 → 0 px.
- **now built-in: Z-C1 radial motion blur** → `camera_presets.crash-zoom.blur {kind: radial, amount 0.18, at face, frames 6, shape decay}` on the fast f1–f4 frames (v03 @7.27). Was: the camera's vertical `vb` blur. Amount 0.18 is the engine example, not measured (not re-measured: budget).
- **now built-in: P-20 type-on** → `VEOS.fx.typeOn` (cps 20 = 1.5 f per glyph, frames 2, drop 0.18 em ≈ 20 px at 112 px, stretch 0.4 = scaleY 1.4 → 1.0), v01 @14.67. Was: a bespoke span-per-glyph scene (and the §17.1 recipe showed a 0.6 → 1.12 scale pop instead of the type-on).
- **now built-in: anchors** → `veos track` + scene `anchor {track, offset, scale_with, lost}` for tags, chip, X, icons and glow on moving objects (v01 @0.83, 1.52, 20.1). Was: hand-read keyframes every 0.5 s, eased. Fallback: `static` from the `--look` frame when there is no trackable object; `lost: fade` or end on the cut when the object leaves.
- **now built-in: P-51 / GR-green** → `flash` transition, colour `good`, peak 0.6, pre 0, frames 10, decay 0.5 (NC-11 counted by core). Was: z11 `good` fill scene. Note: core's flash is a normal-blend fill, the old scene used `mix-blend-mode: color`; the green flash itself was not re-measured (v04 @0:12).
- Still a workaround: P-25 object glow (radial ellipse; needs an object mask, ER-3), GR-bw (z11 saturation scene; ER-5). Caption `reveal: "char"` is **not** applied: no measured caption in this style types on per character (CS-1 hard swap, CS-2/3 word pop are measured); the per-glyph device is the money tag, now `fx.typeOn`.
