Monochrome Metaphor

Inspired by Dan Koe. A voice-over essay becomes one glowing, black-and-white metaphor that morphs scene to scene with no cuts; a single white dot stands for "you"; captions whisper in small type at the bottom. The seated format opens on a text-only black card, then holds one still take with gold lowercase serif captions.
