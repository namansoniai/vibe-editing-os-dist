Brand Stop-motion Ad

For brands and agencies that already own stop-motion or animated shots. The characters act first with no text, small white two-line captions appear only when someone speaks, the product shows up inside the world (in a hand, at the end of a journey, or on an in-world TV), and the wordmark closes alone on black. Inspired by Chamberlain Coffee's claymation and felt ads. The sets below are illustrations standing in for your own plates.
