## Known gaps
- Three clips only, all from one brand: the template generalises the grammar (plate-first, quiet captions, product in world, wordmark close) and leaves the world, characters and product to the buyer's plates.
- Shot changes inside v03 0:01–0:12 are soft (rack focus, flash cuts) and were partly missed by the detector; shot lengths there are read from the 1 fps sheet.
- 720×1280 sources: sizes carry ±10 px error after ×1.5 scaling.

## Engine built-ins (2026-10-07, engine cc324ec+)
| Device | Before (workaround) | Now built-in | Measured value now used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orb collapse (T-BLOOM-OUT) | cut on the bloom's peak frame (a momentary grade brightening) | warm `flash` (pre 5) + built-in `iris` transition, `reveal: "next"`, 6 f, feather 40 | bloom 5 f, the trip shrinks into a soft circle over 6 f over the next plate (v03 @7.08–7.40) |
| Glow-in with radial zoom blur (T-GLOW-IN) | a 3 f warm grade swell on a hard cut, no zoom blur | warm `flash` (pre 3, 15 f) + `timeline.blur` radial envelope (6 f, decay) | 3 f swell, 6 f radial blur, 12 f decay (v03 @5.55–5.67) |
| Stepped in-world animation | each scene quantised its own time (`floor(n / step) × step`) | scene field `step_frames` (2 wordmark, 3 packs / clouds) | screen objects on 2–3 f steps (v02 @8.55–9.00, @15.30–15.87) |
| Moving screen plate / product label | a moving screen plate could not host the spot | `veos track` + scene `anchor` (§17), with a locked-off-take / drop-the-label fallback | — (v02's TV is locked off; offered for buyers' plates) |
| Pack turntable | not available | optional P-SCREEN-PACK3D (`fx.three`, inside the screen only) | — (not in the evidence; optional, N2 still bans full-frame pack shots) |

Still not built-in: the **clay pack "build"** (a ring that morphs into the pack, v02 @8.55–9.00) is object animation: best as the brand's own plate; the engine fallback stays P-PACK-IN's stepped poses. The **end iris** (P-IRIS-OUT: close to r 510, hold, snap to 0) stays a z11 scene: the built-in `iris` has no partial close and hold.
