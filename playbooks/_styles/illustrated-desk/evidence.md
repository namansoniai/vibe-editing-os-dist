## Inferred (not directly measurable)
- Exact easing curves and frame counts (fades, springs, draw-ons) are inferred from 6 fps hook sheets and 1 fps body sheets; the motion tokens are TUNE ±15%.
- Fonts: Poppins (pill) matches closely; Anton for the condensed titles; Instrument Serif / EB Garamond stand in for the serif faces; Permanent Marker stands in for the lighter handwriting (no lighter bundled hand font).
- Type voices (sans / serif / airy / round) generalise the per-brand typography of v01 without using brand fonts.
- The make-room offset formula (584 − head top, clamped 220–340) is derived from v01's head positions and the NC-5 top band.

## Known gaps
- **Sound:** not observable in any reel (no transcripts, no audio analysis). The §11 bed and cue moments are a calm default, `(unverified)`.
- **Speech language:** no transcript; speech read from the burned-in pill (word-accurate). English is the default; the Hinglish / Hindi combinations are offered for buyers and are untested against this style's evidence.
- **3D renders** (v02 plates, bulb, books) cannot be produced by the engine; the template substitutes cut-out photos, line icons and CSS-tilted created covers.
- **Logos and brand UIs** (v01 logos, v03 Slack-like UI, v02 Google-Calendar-like UI) are replaced by creator-supplied files or generic, labelled recreations (NC-6, NC-7).
- **Tracking:** ink on moving footage targets (anchors module) is not available; ink sits on created cards, creator screenshots and static positions only.

## Now built-in (engine packages, cc324ec+)
| Device | Was | Now built-in | Measured value used |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-10 curtain wipe (v03 @ 21.9–22.45) | z1 `fx.clip` in a bespoke scene with a growing top-anchored clip rect | `timeline.transitions` `{"type": "curtain", "dir": "down", "slide": true, "frames": 15}` | 15 f, panel slides down from the top; the engine's curtain ease stands in for the measured ease-out (half the travel in 6 f) |
| T-02 blur-out, then cut (v02 @ 43.27–43.50) | z11 bespoke blur + darken scene | one `timeline.grades` event: `grade: {brightness: 0.85}`, `blur: 20`, `fade: 0.2`, from cut − 0.2 to cut + 0.2, + a transition marker on the cut | blur ≈ 20 px, darken ≈ 15 %, 6 f, hard cut |
| P-18 question card grows with its typed text (v02 @ 43.50–44.38) | bespoke box stepped from the measured text width | `fx.card({grow: "fit-text", typeAt, cps: 45, minW: 140, w: 760})` for one-line questions | 1–2 letters / f (45 cps), grows from the left edge up to 760 px |
| T-11 insert push, P-08 window, P-07 tool drop (v01 @ 9.56, 20.28) | `slide-r` / `slide-l` with the preset's default length and isotropic blur | same presets with `in_frames` / `out_frames` 8 (window in 9, rocket out 4) and `smear: true` (directional smear along the travel) | 8–9 f, ≈ 700 px; presets are measured at rest, so G3 accepts the travel |
| T-06 stack step (v02 @ 15.88–16.32) | stepped `translateY`, no blur primitive | same step (50 px < G3's 90 px / frame needs no exception) + `ctx.blur(8, 90)` vertical smear on the jump frame | 1 f, ≈ 50 px, 2–3 per row |

Still a workaround: **G-1 / G-2 make-room zoom** (≈ 0.89× / 1.12× onto real headroom): the `low` layout has no scale and no real-pixel reveal, and camera v2 cannot zoom below the base framing during a stage change. **Multi-line P-18 growth** (the box also grows taller): `grow: "fit-text"` grows one nowrap line's width only, so longer questions keep the bespoke stepped box.
