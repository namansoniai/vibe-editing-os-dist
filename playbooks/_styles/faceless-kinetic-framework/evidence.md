## Known gaps and what was inferred
- **No transcripts:** the speech language and the spoken lines are inferred from on-screen text. `profile.language.speech` is `(unverified)`.
- **Sound:** not observable. The §11 contract (calm bed from f0, cues on flips / reveals / list cue / CTA) is a decision, not evidence.
- **Fonts:** closest bundled families (Montserrat ExtraBold, identified 2026-10-06; Instrument Serif italic, Anton with `scaleX(0.8)`, Inter Tight). The evidence's condensed face is narrower than Anton.
- **Hub label sizes:** evidence labels are ≈ 20 px at 1× (readable only when zoomed); raised to 40 px world for NC-4 / V-CANVAS.
- **Giant initials** touch the left edge in v03; kept at x 64 because E5 is blocked for faceless profiles (PV-9).
- **Example clips** in v02 are third-party reels with their own audio; the template uses creator-supplied clips muted, or created substitutes.
- **Brown vs black**: v01 uses both a brown world and a pure-black beat; v03 uses charcoal. Modelled as TH-umber, TH-ink (`#141414`) and the P-08 black punch.
- **v02 has no CTA** (possibly truncated); the CTA is taken from v01 and v03.
- **Hook archetype:** STYLE-COVERAGE names HA-06 (HA-12); HA-02 is added from v01's f0 (proof card + headline stack).
